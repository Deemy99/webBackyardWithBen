Our Story

Your Backyard Should Be More Than Just the Space Behind Your House.

A backyard can be a place to relax, grow something, cook dinner, spend time with family or simply enjoy a quiet evening outside.

Backyard with Ben was created to bring all of those ideas together in one place. From simple lawn and garden tips to bigger projects like patios, decks, fences and outdoor kitchens, our goal is to make backyard improvement feel useful, achievable and enjoyable.

You don't need the biggest yard, the biggest budget or years of experience. Sometimes a few smart changes can completely transform the way you use your outdoor space.
